# Enable Contact Center historical analytics reports

Configure Contact Center historical analytics to provide supervisors and operations managers insights into contact center performance. Historical analytics includes the Conversation, Queue, Agent, and Voice insights dashboards.

Contact Center historical analytics reports are built on the Shared Analytics Platform.

Dashboard metrics are available within 24 hours after you enable historical analytics.

## Prerequisites

You must have one of the following roles: System Administrator, Omnichannel Administrator, Omnichannel Supervisor, or CSR Manager.

## Enable Contact Center historical analytics

Enable Contact Center historical analytics in Copilot Service admin center to make historical analytics reports available.

1. In the site map of Copilot Service admin center, under **Operations**, select **Insights**.
1. On the **Insights** page, expand **Report settings**.
1. For **Contact Center historical analytics**, select **Manage**. The Contact Center historical analytics page appears.
1. Turn on the **Enable Contact Center historical analytics** toggle.
1. Select **Save**.

### Enable historical analytics for voice

On the **Contact Center historical analytics** page, under **Additional features**, select **Add historical analytics for voice**, and then select **Save**.
