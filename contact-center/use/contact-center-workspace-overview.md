# Contact Center workspace overview (preview)

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

Use Contact Center workspace to manage voice and messaging conversations in Dynamics 365 Contact Center. You can review assigned conversations, respond to customers, view customer records, consult with other representatives, and use Copilot for suggested replies and conversation summaries. This experience helps you respond faster, stay focused on the conversation, and spend more time helping customers.

[!INCLUDE [preview-note](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-note-d365.md)]

Use the workspace as a standalone experience or within your customer relationship management (CRM) system.

## Understand workspace components

Contact Center workspace brings your assigned work, customer interactions, and assistance into one experience. You can move between conversations without losing customer context, and unsent drafts stay with their conversations.

The representative experience is organized around four primary widgets:

| Widget | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Find and prioritize conversations that need your attention. |
| **Conversation control** | Manage customer interactions, from accepting a conversation through completing follow-up work. |
| **Conversation details** | Keep customer information, notes, and insights available alongside the conversation. |
| **Copilot** | Get AI-assisted answers, suggestions, and guidance while helping customers. |

## Find and prioritize customer conversations

Use **Inbox** to review assigned, open, and completed conversations. Conversation cards display information such as the customer, channel, latest message, and an unread indicator to help you understand your workload.

Search, filter, and sort conversations to focus on the work that matters. You can open an assigned conversation or preview an open chat before assigning it to yourself. Customer-wait indicators help you identify messaging conversations that need a response.

Your presence indicates your availability for work. Your administrator configures how incoming conversations are offered or assigned to you, including whether you need to accept them manually.

## Handle customer conversations

Use **Conversation control** to manage messaging and voice interactions. Depending on the channel and your configuration, you can use quick replies, rich messages, attachments, and real-time translation for chats, or hold, mute, and keypad controls for voice calls. Outbound calling is available if your administrator configures it.

When you need help, consult with another representative or transfer the conversation. Voice calls can also support consultation and transfer to Microsoft Teams users or external numbers if your administrator enables these destinations.

After the customer interaction ends, complete your notes and any required disposition information, and then select **Finish & Close** to complete your session. Ending a customer conversation and leaving only your session are different actions: leaving your session doesn't end the customer conversation.

## View customer and conversation details

Use **Conversation details** to review customer information, survey responses, conversation context, and skills when available. With the required permissions, you can link or update a customer record and save conversation notes.

For completed conversations, review the available transcript and read-only details from the inbox. Voice recordings, call metrics, and conversation journey information depend on your permissions and the data captured for the call.

## Use Copilot

Use **Copilot** to get answers from your organization's knowledge sources and access suggested replies, actions, and resolution guidance. These capabilities depend on the features your administrator enables.

Conversation summaries help you understand the customer issue without rereading the entire interaction. Summaries appear in **Conversation details** > **Copilot insights**. You can also be open them from the Copilot pane. Voice summaries require transcript text.

Review AI-generated content for accuracy and completeness before you use it or share it with a customer. Inserting a suggested reply into a chat doesn't send it, so you can review and edit it first.

## Customize the workspace

Adjust the workspace's appearance, interface language, time zone, and notification sounds in **Global settings**. You can also resize panes and use keyboard shortcuts to move between conversations and tools.

For voice, choose and test your microphone and speaker in the workspace. If the desktop companion application is connected, manage those audio settings in the companion application instead.
