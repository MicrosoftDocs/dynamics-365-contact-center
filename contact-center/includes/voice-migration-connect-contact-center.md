## Connect the migrated numbers to Dynamics 365 Contact Center

Each service phone number that you use with Dynamics 365 Contact Center requires a Microsoft Teams resource account.

1. [Create a Microsoft Teams resource account](../administer/configure-teams-phone-in-voice-channel.md#create-a-teams-resource-account).

1. [Assign a license to the resource account](/microsoftteams/aa-cq-manage-resource-accounts#assign-a-license).
1. [Assign the migrated phone number to the resource account](../administer/configure-teams-phone-in-voice-channel.md#assign-service-number-to-teams-resource-account). Use the assignment method for Teams Calling Plan, Direct Routing, or Operator Connect, based on your migration path.
1. [Sync the phone number and configure the voice channel](../administer/configure-teams-phone-in-voice-channel.md#configure-teams-phone-in-the-voice-channel).
