# Use coaching and development (preview)

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

As a representative, you can view contextual coaching tips, review and respond to published scorecards, and complete assigned training in Copilot Service workspace. You don't need access to the Workforce Engagement Management (preview) app.

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-note-d365.md)]

## Prerequisites

- An administrator enabled coaching and development for your organization.
- A supervisor published coaching instructions, scorecards, or training for your business unit.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This feature is intended to help customer service managers or supervisors enhance their team's performance and improve customer satisfaction. It isn't intended to be used, and shouldn't be used, to make decisions that affect the employment of an employee or group of employees, including compensation, rewards, seniority, or other rights or entitlements.
>
> Customers are solely responsible for using Dynamics 365, this feature, and any associated feature or service in compliance with all applicable laws, including laws that are related to accessing individual employee analytics, and monitoring, recording, and storing communications with users. As part of this compliance, customers must adequately notify users that their communications with customer service representatives (service representatives or representatives) might be monitored, recorded, or stored. As required by applicable laws, customers must also obtain consent from users before they use this feature with them. In addition, customers are encouraged to have a mechanism in place to inform their service representatives that their communications with users might be monitored, recorded, or stored.

## Review a scorecard

1. In Copilot Service workspace, select **My scorecard** in **Service**.
1. On the **Coaching & Development** page, select **Scorecards**. Review your latest published scorecard. To view earlier scorecards, select **Previous scorecards**.
1. Review the **Coaching overview**, quantitative data, and insights about what you're doing well and your opportunities for growth. To learn how an insight was generated, select **About this insight**.
1. Complete one of the following actions:
   - Select **Acknowledge scorecard** to confirm that you received and reviewed it.
   - Enter **Comments for manager**, and then select **Return with comments** to send feedback and reopen the scorecard for your supervisor.

> [!NOTE]
> Acknowledging a scorecard confirms that you reviewed it. It doesn't indicate agreement and doesn't complete associated training.

## Complete assigned training

1. On the **Coaching & Development** page, select **Training** to open **My training**.
1. Review the **Training type**, **Requirement** (**Required** or **Recommended**), **Due date**, **Status**, **Progress**, and **Actions**.
1. Select **Start** to begin training or **Resume** to continue it.
1. To review completed training, select **View**.

Training might include:

- **Knowledge article review**: Review assigned knowledge articles.
- **Knowledge check**: Complete a quiz generated from source knowledge articles.
- **Scenario-based training**: Practice a simulated customer conversation and review feedback from the AI coach.

Scenario-based training is completed when the practice ends. It isn't scored as pass or fail.

## AI-generated content

AI can generate real-time tips, scorecard insights, and training content.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> AI-generated content might be incorrect. Review the content and use your judgment before applying it. Scores can reflect interactions in which multiple people participated and might not be based only on your individual contribution.
