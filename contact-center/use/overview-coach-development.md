# Overview of coaching and development (preview)

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

Coaching and development is a Quality management capability that uses Quality Evaluation Agent to provide contextual coaching to representatives, generate scorecards from completed evaluations, surface team-level coaching insights, and support targeted training.

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-note-d365.md)]

Coaching and development helps your organization:

- Provide representatives with contextual guidance while they work on cases.
- Create periodic scorecards that combine quantitative metrics and AI-generated insights.
- Identify team trends and opportunities for growth.
- Create, assign, and track targeted training.
- Review changes in quality before and after coaching and training.

## How coaching and development works

Coaching and development includes the following components:

- **Scorecards**: Weekly or monthly scorecards summarize quantitative data and AI-generated insights. Supervisors can review scorecards before publishing them to representatives.
- **Coaching dashboard**: Supervisors can review scorecards that need attention, team quality scores, and AI-generated coaching insights.
- **Training**: Supervisors can create, publish, assign, and track knowledge article reviews, knowledge checks, and scenario-based training.
- **Impact**: Supervisors can review observed quality changes before coaching, after training completion, and during a follow-up period.

## AI-generated content

Coaching and development uses generative AI to create coaching tips, coaching insights, scorecard overviews and insights, training recommendations, and training content.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> AI-generated content might be incorrect. Review AI-generated content for accuracy and appropriateness before you use or publish it. Scores can reflect interactions in which multiple people participated and might not be directly attributable to one representative. Changes displayed in the impact view are observed and don't prove that coaching or training caused the change.

Human review and control are built into the experience:

- Representatives decide whether to apply real-time coaching tips and can rate them as **Helpful** or **Not helpful**.
- Supervisors can modify default coaching instructions before publishing them.
- Scorecards can require supervisor review before AI-generated insights are shared.
- Supervisors review, edit, and test generated training before publishing it.
